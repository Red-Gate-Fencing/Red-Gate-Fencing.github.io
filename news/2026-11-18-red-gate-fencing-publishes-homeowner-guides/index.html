Red Gate Fencing Publishes Homeowner Guides on Fence Contractors and Installation

The Camp Hill fence company released four educational guides for property owners comparing contractors, companies, and installation plans in the Harrisburg area.

Red Gate FencingUpdated 2026-11-18Camp Hill, PA
In this guide
  1. About Red Gate Fencing

CAMP HILL, Pa. (November 18, 2026) — Red Gate Fencing, a family-owned fence company based at 118 Yellow Breeches Drive in Camp Hill, has published a set of homeowner guides for people who are hiring a fence contractor, comparing fence companies, or planning a fence installation in the Harrisburg area.

Key facts
  • Red Gate Fencing published four educational guides covering fence contractors, fence companies, fence builders, and fence installation.
  • The guides are written for property owners in Camp Hill, the Harrisburg area, Cumberland County, and nearby communities the company serves.
  • The company installs wood, vinyl, aluminum, chain link, privacy, and farm fencing and offers free, no-obligation estimates with a site visit.
  • Readers can review the guides, visit redgatefencing.com, or find the company on its Google Business Profile.

The new resources are intended as a practical starting point, not a substitute for a visit to the property. Each guide focuses on a different part of the decision. The fence contractor guide covers how to prepare for a site visit, what to compare in an estimate, and questions worth asking any contractor. The fence company guide explains what to expect from a local company, including communication, service area, and the range of materials a complete installer should be able to discuss. The fence builder guide looks at layout, posts, freeze-thaw conditions in Central Pennsylvania, and how the build changes from wood and vinyl to aluminum, chain link, and farm fencing. The fence installation guide walks homeowners through purpose, material choice, permits and property lines, installation day, and follow-up care.

Red Gate Fencing serves homeowners, businesses, and agricultural property owners throughout Harrisburg and surrounding communities within a 50-mile radius, including work across Cumberland, Dauphin, and York counties and additional communities in Perry and Lebanon counties. The company is owned and operated by Stephen and Erica Snyder. According to the business, the couple brought more than 25 years of experience in construction and home improvement to the company and organized it around clear communication, straightforward pricing, and installation they are willing to stand behind.

The guides also point readers to the company’s service pages for privacy, wood, vinyl, aluminum, chain link, and farm fence installation, and to its contact and estimating tools. People who want to discuss a specific property can call (717) 319-0765, email stephen@redgatefencing.com, or request a site visit through the website. The company’s Google Business Profile, which lists a 4.9-star rating from 54 reviews, is available at share.google/2kul3TJSfiEbm35Dx.
